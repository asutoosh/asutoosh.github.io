Systems I Reverse-Engineered

  • YouTube giveaway speed system

    I observed recurring winner patterns in IPL-related giveaway ecosystems and identified it as a timing problem. I learned Python and used the YouTube API to detect uploads and comment instantly.

    Over three IPL seasons this led to multiple wins including gadgets and cash-equivalent rewards.

  • Middleman distribution arbitrage

    Instead of building a new platform, I connected creators needing visibility with niche channels that already had audience fit.

    Generated about $500 in commissions across roughly two months.

Current Big Bet: Dupages

Dupages connects PG owners in Delhi with students moving in for college admissions. I mapped 90+ Delhi University colleges and designed matching around a 300-meter radius to reduce search friction for incoming students.

  • Demand context: CUET pipeline and student migration make this a recurring annual market.
  • Execution in progress: owner supply onboarding via calling agent + WhatsApp automation.
  • Growth layer: content and SEO engine to capture student demand.
  • Business intent: this is a scale-focused build, not a side project.
