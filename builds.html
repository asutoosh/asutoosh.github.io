how i got here

Not a résumé. Just the real sequence — what I hacked, what I built, what failed, and what started making money.

Hack

YouTube giveaway mechanics

Some IPL fantasy creators reward the first commenter on new videos and announce it in their Telegram groups beforehand. I noticed the same accounts winning repeatedly, so I reverse-engineered the system.

It was purely a speed problem. I learned basic Python and built a script using the YouTube API to detect uploads and comment instantly.

Result: Over three IPL seasons, I consistently placed among the first commenters. Won earbuds, smartwatches, a smartphone, and a few hundred dollars total.
Hack

Being the middleman

I noticed creators wanted visibility, and some niche websites already had the right audience. Instead of building something new, I just connected both sides.

I partnered with Hadzy.com and helped a creator promote through it. By coordinating both sides, I generated around $500 in commissions over two months.

Failed

upcmts.com — first SaaS attempt

I wanted to turn the commenting hack into a product. Built a WordPress-based site called upcmts.com where I offered a "first comment service" on YouTube giveaways. The idea was simple: speed wins rewards.

The domain expired (Nov 2025). It failed mainly because I didn't understand distribution at that time.

Lesson: Distribution matters more than just building. A useful product still fails without a channel.

Revenue

Product

Works across forex and crypto. ~40% win rate with 1:5 risk-reward. Built a small community of 40+ traders using it daily.

Result: Generated $5K+ revenue. Planning to monetize it properly.

Scale

Dupgs.com — the big bet

Right now, the most important thing I'm building is Dupgs. A platform connecting PG owners in Delhi with students coming to the city for college.

Every year, around 1.3 million students appear for CUET, and roughly 70,000 move to Delhi. Most of them struggle to find PG accommodation near their colleges. I mapped 90+ Delhi University colleges and built a system that connects them with PGs within a 300-meter radius.

Current focus: Supply onboarding via calling agent + WhatsApp automation. Co-founder working on the app side. Content + SEO to capture demand. Targeting $400K/year business.
